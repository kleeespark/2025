SPARK FEST

Welcome to SPARK FEST – the official Department Fest celebrating innovation, technology, creativity, and teamwork! This exciting event brings together students, technical enthusiasts, and creative minds for an unforgettable experience.

SPARK FEST features a wide range of technical and non-technical events including coding challenges, circuit design, project expos, technical quizzes, workshops, gaming, photography, cultural activities, and fun competitions that inspire learning and collaboration.

Join us to showcase your talent, enhance your skills, compete with passionate participants, and connect with future innovators. Be a part of this energetic celebration where ideas come alive, creativity shines, and technology meets entertainment. Let’s ignite innovation and create unforgettable memories together! 🚀
